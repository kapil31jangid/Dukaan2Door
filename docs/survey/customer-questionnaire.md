# Customer Survey: Local Grocery Ordering and Delivery

**Estimated time:** 3–5 minutes  
**Audience:** Potential customers who buy groceries or household essentials

## Consent

Dukaan2Door is an academic project studying local-store ordering and delivery.
Participation is voluntary. Do not provide passwords, financial details,
government identifiers, or exact home addresses. Responses will be used only
for academic product research and may be reported in aggregate. You may skip
optional questions or stop at any time.

**C0. Do you voluntarily agree to participate?** *(Required, single choice)*

- Yes
- No → End form
