# Shopkeeper Survey: Local Inventory and Delivery

**Estimated time:** 3–5 minutes  
**Audience:** Owners or managers of local kirana/general stores

## Consent

Dukaan2Door is an academic project studying inventory and local-delivery
challenges. Participation is voluntary. Do not provide passwords, financial
account numbers, government identifiers, or customer details. Responses will be
used only for academic product research and may be reported in aggregate. You
may skip any optional question or stop at any time.
